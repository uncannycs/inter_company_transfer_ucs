See It In Action

1. Set Up Transfer Rules & Warehouses

Easily configure how goods move between your companies in Inventory → Settings. Choose whether transfers trigger on Sales Orders, Purchase Orders, or both, select default warehouses, and choose your preferred automation options.

2. Create and Send a Stock Transfer

Start a new transfer right from the Inventory menu. Choose the sending company and warehouse, select the receiving company and warehouse, add items with quantities and prices, and click Process.

3. Easily View Linked Orders & Shipments

After processing, everything stays organized in one place. Use the top buttons to quickly open the connected Sales Order, Purchase Order, and Shipments, or use the Reverse button if you ever need to return the goods.

4. Return Goods with One Click

Need to return goods back to the sending company? Simply click Reverse. The app automatically creates a return transfer in the opposite direction, keeping inventory and orders balanced across both companies.

5. Automatic Sales Order in the Sending Company

The sending company automatically creates a confirmed Sales Order for the receiving sister company. It stays directly linked to the transfer so your team can easily check delivery progress.

6. Automatic Purchase Order in the Receiving Company

At the same time, the receiving company automatically gets a Purchase Order from the sending company. Your team can view and track incoming warehouse receipts directly from the order.

7. Optional Invoicing & Delivery Automation

For a hands-free experience, enable full automation in Settings. Stock deliveries and receipts will validate automatically, and customer invoices and vendor bills will be generated without manual effort.

8. Complete Multi-Company Billing & Accounting

When billing automation is active, the app automatically creates matching invoices and vendor bills. You can access all connected sales orders, purchase orders, shipments, and bills right from the transfer screen.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about the Inter-Company Inventory Transfer module.

1. What is the main purpose of this system?

It automates and simplifies the transfer of stock between different business branches and sister entities. When inventory moves from one location to another, the system automatically creates and confirms the matching sales orders, purchase orders, delivery slips, incoming receipts, customer invoices, and vendor bills. This eliminates repetitive manual data entry, prevents stock count errors, and ensures complete financial and inventory alignment.

2. How do I start a transfer between two entities?

Transfers can be initiated in two ways: through a dedicated transfer record where you select the source and destination entities and process the movement directly, or automatically by simply confirming a standard sales order or purchase order created for a sister entity.

3. How does automatic order synchronization work?

When a sales order is confirmed in the selling branch, the system automatically generates and confirms a matching purchase order in the purchasing branch. Similarly, confirming a purchase order can automatically create and confirm the reciprocal sales order, ensuring both sides stay perfectly aligned.

4. Can I choose whether automation triggers from sales or purchase orders?

Yes. You can customize transaction rules per entity based on your preferred workflow. You can set the system to trigger transfers exclusively from sales orders, exclusively from purchase orders, or in both directions.

5. How does the system update warehouse inventory levels?

During a transfer, the system generates an outgoing delivery order from the dispatching warehouse and a matching incoming receipt at the receiving warehouse. Once validated, stock quantities are accurately deducted from the source location and credited to the destination location.

6. Can warehouse delivery orders and incoming receipts be validated automatically?

Yes. You can enable automatic validation for shipments and receipts. When activated, the system immediately registers product dispatch and receipt without requiring warehouse staff to manually validate each document.

7. How does the system handle financial invoicing and vendor bills?

When a transfer is processed, the system can automatically create a customer invoice for the sending entity and a matching vendor bill for the receiving entity, keeping inter-branch receivables and payables balanced.

8. Can invoices and vendor bills be posted automatically?

Yes. The system includes an option to automatically validate and post generated invoices and bills immediately upon creation, saving accounting teams time while still allowing the flexibility to leave them in draft status if managerial verification is preferred.

9. What happens if products need to be returned to the original branch?

You can execute an instant return using the built-in reversal feature. With a single click on a completed transfer, the system automatically creates a mirror return transfer that swaps the source and destination entities and generates the corresponding return documents.

10. Can I set default dispatch and receiving warehouses for each branch?

Yes. You can configure a default transfer warehouse for each branch. When creating a transfer, the system automatically selects these preferred locations for both dispatch and receipt, accelerating entry and preventing misrouted stock.

11. How can users track all related documents for a transfer?

Every transfer record features interactive shortcut buttons that provide direct access to all associated sales orders, purchase orders, shipments, receipts, and invoices, giving your team a complete end-to-end audit trail.

12. Can I transfer multiple products at different agreed prices in one request?

Yes. You can add multiple product lines with distinct quantities, units of measure, and custom unit transfer prices in a single transfer document, and these values will automatically carry over across all generated transactions.

13. How does the system handle branches operating in different currencies?

The system smoothly supports cross-currency transactions. Prices and totals are accurately maintained according to the currency defined on the transfer, ensuring multi-currency financial records remain precise.

14. How are user access permissions and security managed?

The system provides dedicated permission levels: Standard Users can create, view, and process transfers, while Managers have full authority to configure automation rules, default warehouses, and system settings.

15. Can I print physical summary reports for warehouse and shipping records?

Yes. Clean, professional printable summary reports can be generated for both outgoing transfers and return shipments, detailing transfer references, locations, items, quantities, and pricing for physical verification.
